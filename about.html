About Global B2B Trading

Global B2B Trading is an early-stage online marketplace project designed to help business buyers and suppliers discover product opportunities and exchange commercial quotations across countries.

How the marketplace works

  • Suppliers can create product listings with category, origin, minimum order quantity and an indicative price.
  • Buyers can browse active listings and send a request for quotation (RFQ) with quantity, target price and specifications.
  • Buyers can also publish an open requirement so eligible sellers can submit private bids.
  • The bid workflow includes private messages between the buyer who posted the requirement and the seller who submitted the bid.
  • For product RFQs, the current workflow provides a seller reply field; it is not yet a full two-way chat system.

Seller membership

Sellers can browse open buyer requirements without a paid membership. An active, administrator-approved membership is required to submit bids and privately message buyers.

  • Monthly membership: US$10
  • Yearly membership: US$100

Membership payment is currently handled manually through bKash or Nagad Send Money. Sellers submit a transaction reference for administrator review. PayPal is not currently available, and the website does not automatically collect or reconcile membership payments.

Important limitations

Global B2B Trading does not currently provide independent buyer or seller identity verification, escrow, order fulfillment, shipping/logistics, dispute resolution or automatic payment processing. Membership approval confirms a membership payment; it is not a certification of a business or its products.

Listings, requirements, bids and messages are intended to support initial business discussions. They are not a guarantee that another party is legitimate, that a product meets a specification, or that a transaction will be completed. Businesses should independently verify counterparties, samples, product specifications, payment instructions and written delivery terms before sending money or goods.
