Admin — Buyer & Seller Verification

Documents are NOT mandatory. Review the business name, country, profile details, products or requirements, and—when needed—contact details or a company website from Admin User Management. You may contact the account directly to confirm. Optional trade license or other documents may be checked if voluntarily provided outside this dashboard. Only mark Verified when you have reasonably confirmed the business identity. Verified badges are public; private email, phone and payment information remain hidden.

Verification checklist (lightweight)
  • Business/company name and country make sense
  • Profile, product or buyer requirement details look credible
  • Contact/email/website checked if available; contact directly if uncertain
  • Documents are optional—not required for verification
